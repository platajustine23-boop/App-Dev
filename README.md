## My Favorite Series: Stranger Things

**Stranger Things** is a science fiction and horror series created by the Duffer Brothers. It is set in the 1980s and follows a group of kids as they uncover supernatural mysteries in their small town.

### Why I Like It
- Engaging storyline with lots of suspense  
- Strong friendship between characters  
- Great mix of horror, sci-fi, and nostalgia  

### Main Characters
- Eleven  
- Mike Wheeler  
- Dustin Henderson  
- Lucas Sinclair  

### Favorite Moment
One of my favorite moments is when Eleven uses her powers to save her friends—it’s both intense and emotional.
